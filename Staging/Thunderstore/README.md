# VitalShift
A BoneLab mod that swaps your avatar based on your health. <br>
Last tested on `Patch 6` <br>

## Settings
**Set High/Medium/Low avatar:** <br>
Pressing this button sets your **currently equipped avatar** to the the pressed health tier. <br>
VitalShift will set your avatar to the selected avatar on high/medium/low based on your health, <br>
so, 'High' is your 'default' avatar, 'medium' and 'low' are the avatars you will change into when you get damaged. <br>

**HP Tiers**:
- High: More than 70%
- Medium: Between 70% and 30%
- Low: 30% and below
*All tiers default to Ford if you have not selected the avatar for that tier manually.* <br>

**Useful note:** <br>
If you equip an avatar that is not either the high/medium/low avatar, the mod will disable itself. <br>
This way you can still use any avatar without the mod forcing you back. <br>
