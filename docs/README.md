# VitalShift
A BoneLab mod that swaps your avatar as your health drops, allowing for visual damage with avatar variants. <br>
Last tested on `Patch 6` <br>

## Settings
Set High/Medium/Low avatar: <br>
Pressing this button sets your **currently equipped avatar** to the the pressed health tier. <br>
VitalShift will set your avatar to the selected avatar on high/medium/low based on your health, <br>
so, 'High' is your 'default' avatar, 'medium' and 'low' are the avatars you will change into when you get damaged. <br>

HP Tiers:
- High: More than 70%
- Medium: Between 70% and 30%
- Low: 30% and below
<br>

All tiers default to Ford if you have not selected the avatar for that tier manually.
